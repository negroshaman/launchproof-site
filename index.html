The audit path

One journey, checked end to end.

LaunchProof follows the buying path a real shopper takes. The scope stays focused so the findings are useful, not buried in a generic checklist.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Homepage, navigation, search and collection browsing.

  2. 02

    Decide

    Product information, variants, availability and key choices.

  3. 03

    Add to cart

    Cart actions, totals, edits, messaging and state.

  4. 04

    Safe-stop

    Checkout entry is reviewed. No order is placed and no payment is captured.

FAQ

Useful details before you ask.

Where does the audit stop?

At checkout entry. LaunchProof does not place an order, submit payment or capture payment details.

What access do you need?

A public store usually needs only its URL. Password-protected or test stores may need temporary, limited access. Any requirement is agreed before the audit starts.

When does the 48–72 hour window begin?

After the agreed scope, store access and necessary context are ready. Timing is confirmed before work begins.

Do you fix the issues?

The pilot is an audit and report. Implementation is outside the standard scope, so there is no pressure to buy a redesign or ongoing engagement.

Do you guarantee a sales increase?

No. The audit documents observed journey issues and opportunities. It does not promise conversion or revenue results.

What does “Phase 0 pilot” mean?

LaunchProof is validating a focused service with a limited number of early stores. The process is real; the claims stay deliberately modest.
