LaunchProof

A focused Shopify Store Journey Audit.

LaunchProof reviews the path shoppers take from discovery through checkout — on desktop and mobile — and stops before any order or payment.

What it is

A fixed-scope Store Journey Audit for Shopify stores. The deliverable is a prioritized report with evidence and reproduction steps that can be handed to a developer.

Journey scope

  • Home → collection → product page → cart → checkout entry
  • Safe-stop: no purchase, no payment capture
  • Desktop and mobile
  • Findings only from observed evidence

What you get

  • Prioritized findings (severity + impact)
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Expected vs actual
  • Evidence notes a developer can act on

Not a redesign pitch. Not a retainer. Not a conversion guarantee.

What it is not

  • Not a full CRO agency engagement
  • Not penetration testing or security exploitation
  • Not guaranteed sales lift
  • Not “case studies” invented for marketing
